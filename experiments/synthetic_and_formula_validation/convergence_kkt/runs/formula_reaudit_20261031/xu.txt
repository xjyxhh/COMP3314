B. Information-Theoretic Metric Learning

  The key idea of ITML is to learn the distance metric M
by enforcing that the learned distance dM  is large for the
dissimilar pairs of samples and small for the similar pairs
of samples. In particular, they expect d2M(xi, x j) ≤u for a
relatively small value u  if (i, j) ∈S, and d2M(xi, x j) ≥l
for a sufﬁciently large l if (i, j) ∈D. However, for the real-
world applications, a feasible solution may not exist after using
those strict constraints. Thus, a slack variable ξij is introduced
